# Automated Communication

AI agents can do more than just write code; they can also bridge the communication gap between developers and stakeholders.

## Automating Updates

Use the agent to automatically parse your Git commit history and draft communications such as:
- **Release Notes**: Summarizing the technical changes into readable feature logs.
- **Stakeholder Updates**: Drafting Microsoft Teams or Slack update messages to keep project managers and business owners informed of your progress automatically.
