
## What is a Personal Developer Instance (PDI)?
ServiceNow provides FREE, fully functional Personal Developer Instances (PDIs) to registered users. A PDI is a sandbox environment where you can safely learn, practice, and build without affecting production systems.

Your PDI stays active as long as you use it regularly. If inactive, it goes into sleep/hibernate mode — just wake it up before using again.

* Completely free for registered ServiceNow Developer Program members.
* Fully functional — same as a real ServiceNow instance.
* Perfect for practising and learning all ServiceNow features.
* Only ONE developer account per Gmail address allowed.
* Current UI in use: UI16.

> **IMPORTANT:** If not used regularly, the instance hibernates — wake it up before use.

## Step-by-Step: Setting Up Your PDI

### 1. Visit the ServiceNow Developer Site
Go to: [https://developer.servicenow.com/](https://developer.servicenow.com/)  
This is the official ServiceNow developer portal.

### 2. Sign Up for a ServiceNow ID
Click "Sign up and Start Building" on the homepage.
Fill in: First name, Last name, Email (use your Gmail), Country, Username, Password.
Complete the CAPTCHA and accept the terms, then click Sign Up.

### 3. Verify Your Email
You will receive a "Thank You" confirmation on screen.
Check your Gmail inbox for an email from ServiceNow.
Open it and click the "Verify Email" button.

### 4. Complete the Getting Started Setup
After verification, you'll be redirected to a setup questionnaire.
Select "Yes — I need a developer oriented IDE" (if you code).
Choose your job responsibilities (Developer, Designer, IT Admin, etc.).
Agree to the ServiceNow Developer site terms of use.
Click "Finish Setup".

### 5. Request Your Developer Instance
On the developer portal, click "Request Instance" (top-right button).
ServiceNow will provision your personal instance.
You will see: Your instance URL, Username (admin), Temporary Password.

### 6. Open Your Instance
Click "Open Instance" to launch your ServiceNow home page.
Click Next -> Next -> Next -> Got It to dismiss the welcome tour.
You are now inside your own fully functional ServiceNow instance!

## How to Check Your ServiceNow Version
To find out which version of ServiceNow your instance is running:

1. Open the Application Navigator (left sidebar).
2. Type `stats.do` in the filter/search bar and press Enter.
3. The Servlet Statistics page shows your build name, version, and instance details.

> **TIP:** Always use the latest available version of ServiceNow for the best features and security.
