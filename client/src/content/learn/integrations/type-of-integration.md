Integration basically two type

-   **Inbound Integration**
    -   Inbound Integration Means any data I am receiving by third-party involvement. The trigger is happening from a third party and they are consuming my end to send the data that’s called inbound. Once I am receiving it, my system will process and perform certain actions.
-   **Outbound Integration**
    -   In outbound, my system will consume any third-party endpoint and trigger based on my system condition that is called outbound.
