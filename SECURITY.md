## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover any security related issues, please email the maintainers instead of using the issue tracker. All security vulnerabilities will be promptly addressed.

We will acknowledge receipt of your vulnerability report and strive to send you regular updates about our progress. If you have not received a reply to your email within 48 hours, or have not heard from us for a week, please reach out again.
